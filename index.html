How it works

Three simple steps for a teacher or a student.

1

Pick a language

Choose the child's mother tongue from the list.

2

Add the lesson

Type or paste a line from the textbook.

3

Read and listen

See the translation and a simple explanation with a local example.

What the tool offers

Real-time translation

Textbook sentences appear in the child's language as soon as they are entered.

Teaching in the mother tongue

Ideas are explained with words and examples children know from home and village life.

Made for primary classes

Short sentences, big letters, and simple words for Classes 1 to 5.

Helps teachers too

Teachers can prepare bilingual worksheets in minutes.
